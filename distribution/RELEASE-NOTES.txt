Civilization 0.2.14 / build 16

- Town Hall, Community Organization research, influence, policies, birth policies and configurable emergency food orders.
- Happiness affects civilian work efficiency; per-building construction labor caps.
- Individual pregnancy and recovery exclude people from available workforce.
- Research points with 40–60 point foundational technologies and School daily-output bonuses.
- Starting camp objects block placement until builders dismantle them.
- Army training reserves, army creation/editing, assigned home defense and messenger-delivered field orders.
- Up to 16 civilizations, late joining and mixed Easy/Medium/Hard AI counts.
- Terrain-following connected fields, scoutable dry riverbanks and clearer winter daylight hex lines.
- Food HUD rate reflects net stock change after consumption and spoilage.

Mac and Windows packages and signed content updates. Gameplay, live updater installation and cross-machine multiplayer have not been tested for this release.
