Build options visibility
Fixed a nested tab layout issue that hid building options in the Build panel, even when construction was unlocked. Existing saves need no migration.

River and tree visuals
Improved procedural tree crowns and added more visible river banks, shoreline shallows, and animated highlights.

Startup crash fix
Fixed three GDScript type-inference errors that prevented the settlement scene from loading in the 0.2.11 content pack. Existing 0.2.11 users receive the fix through the automatic content update on relaunch.

Build availability guidance
The Build tab explains the initial research needed to unlock construction and how to place buildings once available.


Food production rate
The top bar now shows +X/day beneath Food, using gross production before
consumption and spoilage, matching the daily rates shown for Wood and Stone.

Object inspection click fix
Fixed map clicks failing to open details for some objects, including armies and
other inspectables outside the settlement scene branch. Depleted resource meshes
no longer intercept clicks, and panel overlap uses the click's actual position.

Playtest speeds
Added solo playtest options for one game day per 10 seconds and per 30 seconds.

People tab population counts
The People tab now shows how many children, adults and elders are in the settlement.

Scrollable Command panels
Overview, Build, People, Explore and Military contents now scroll vertically
when they exceed the panel. Building categories and their options scroll too,
while tab headers stay visible.

Bug fix: Command tabs retain selection
Fixed Command tabs briefly opening and then switching away during HUD updates.
Overview, Build, People, Explore and Military now keep the selected page visible.

Update queued during multiplayer
Settings now lets players download updates while a shared world continues, then
queue the restart for after leaving. The game saves before restarting. A host
chooses when to leave and disconnect guests.
