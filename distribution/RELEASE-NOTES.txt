Traveling trade scouts and clearer settlement controls

- Trade offers travel with three scouts and reserved cargo; recipients accept, decline or seize them. AI decisions use resource needs and relations and AI can initiate offers.
- Ready home armies can kill visiting exploration or trade scouts within home influence, taking cargo within storage capacity and damaging relations.
- Clickable army roster, direct editing and troop selectors bounded by available trained units and the 30-soldier limit.
- Enemy badges hide outside live sight; home influence identifies troops and their mission.
- Simpler houses, barracks and town hall; softer fog edges; faint technology arrows with brighter prerequisite and onward paths.
- Corrects a terrain shader identifier collision. The reported native saved-world crash remains unconfirmed.
